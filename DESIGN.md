# Flag Heist landing page

## Overview
The supplied motion reference establishes saturated red, black, large condensed lettering, and an orbit around a central figure. The event banner supplies the red hood, ivory mask, white flag, and factual event information. This system applies to the landing page; the existing arena retains its own styles.

## Colors
Red dominates the hero. Ink grounds the investigation sections; paper supplies readable foreground text and the evidence sheet. Muted text is reserved for supporting information.

## Typography
Self-hosted Barlow Condensed Bold is the display face, named Heist Display in CSS. Geist handles reading and controls. Geist Mono is limited to evidence file metadata. Display headings keep authored line breaks and fit at most two lines.

## Layout
The desktop hero splits large type and a character portrait. Phones stack them. The mission, challenge preview, and field guide alternate type and artifact layouts. Keep the outer dark frame and straight control edges.

## Elevation & Depth
Use flat color fields. The tilted evidence sheet, transparent character, and orbit layering provide depth. No decorative shadows.

## Shapes
A thin elliptical orbit recalls the motion reference. Rectangular controls and fine dividers echo its framed composition.

## Components
The hero uses a generated transparent character with a nine-second perspective turn and moving orbit. The pause control freezes both; reduced-motion preferences disable them. This is an animated portrait, not a 360-degree model. Challenge tabs support arrow keys, Home, and End.

## Do's and Don'ts
Keep event facts faithful to the supplied banner: 12–13 November, hybrid, one to three participants, ₹75/₹100/₹150 per team. Do not invent a prize amount, schedule, testimonials, or participant counts. Keep challenge answers out of landing-page data. Character generation details live in the artwork metadata.
